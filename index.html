OUR STORY

Born on the Street.

Spicy Mafia didn't start in a kitchen — it started with a cart, a dream, and a recipe that stopped people mid-walk in Jagriti Vihar. Every dish we make carries the chaos, colour, and heat of Meerut's streets.

We don't do mild. We don't do boring. We do real — fresh ingredients, bold spices, and portions that actually fill you up.
